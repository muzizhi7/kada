### Preview limitations

No standalone installer, signing/notarization or automatic updates. Windows is unverified. The shell is not sandboxed. Real computer-use and automatic learning are disabled by default. Effective hour-scale reliability and repeatable quality/cost gains over Pi remain unproven.

Development history and frozen experiment results remain in docs/. They predate this first public release and do not imply earlier public releases.
